# Professional Services Agreement

This Professional Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of June 15, 2026 between Northwind Placeholder Consulting Inc. ("Provider") and Example Buyer Co. ("Client"). All names in this document are fictitious.

## 1. Services

Provider will perform the consulting services described in each statement of work (each, an "SOW"). Each SOW is governed by this Agreement.

## 2. Term and Renewal

This Agreement starts on the effective date and continues for two (2) years. It then renews automatically for successive two (2) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the current term.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Client will pay Provider's invoices within fifteen (15) days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 2% per month. Provider may raise its hourly rates once per calendar year on 60 days' written notice.

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice. Provider may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice. Client may terminate an SOW for convenience on ninety (90) days' notice, subject to an early termination fee equal to 25% of the remaining fees under that SOW.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other party's confidential information using at least reasonable care and will use it only to perform this Agreement. These obligations survive for six (6) months after this Agreement ends. Standard exclusions apply to information that is public, already known, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party.

## 6. Intellectual Property

Each party keeps its pre-existing intellectual property. Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use the deliverables for Client's internal business purposes. Provider retains ownership of all deliverables, including those created specifically for Client.

## 7. Data Protection

Provider will process Client personal data only to perform the Services and in accordance with applicable data protection law, and will maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures. Provider will notify Client of a personal data breach within five (5) business days of discovery. At the end of the Agreement, Provider will return or delete Client personal data on request.

## 8. Indemnification

Each party will indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its gross negligence or willful misconduct. Provider will indemnify Client against third-party claims that the deliverables infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Except for indemnification obligations, each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months before the claim. Neither party will be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

## 10. Non-Solicitation

During the term and for twelve (12) months after, neither party will solicit for employment any employee of the other party who worked on the Services, except through general job advertisements.

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

## 12. General

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in the applicable SOW. This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

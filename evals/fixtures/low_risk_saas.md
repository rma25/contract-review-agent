# Software Subscription Agreement

This Software Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 10, 2026 between Brightleaf Placeholder Software Ltd. ("Supplier") and Example Buyer Co. ("Customer"). All names in this document are fictitious.

## 1. Subscription

Supplier grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Supplier platform during the subscription term for Customer's internal business purposes.

## 2. Term and Renewal

The subscription term is twelve (12) months. It renews for successive twelve (12) month terms only if Customer signs a renewal order form. Renewal pricing will not increase by more than 3% over the prior term.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Customer will pay undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt. Customer may dispute an invoice in good faith by notifying Supplier before the due date. Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month. If Supplier fails to provide the platform as agreed, Customer is entitled to a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice. Customer may terminate for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice and will receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused portion of the term.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other party's confidential information with at least reasonable care and use it only to perform this Agreement. These obligations are mutual and survive for three (3) years after termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets. Standard exclusions apply.

## 6. Intellectual Property

Each party retains all rights in its pre-existing intellectual property. Customer owns all Customer data and any reports generated from it. Customer grants Supplier a limited license to use Customer data solely to provide the platform during the term.

## 7. Data Protection

Supplier will process Customer personal data only on Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with the attached data processing addendum. Supplier will maintain appropriate security measures, notify Customer of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours, give Customer 30 days' notice before engaging new subprocessors, and delete or return all Customer data within 30 days after termination.

## 8. Indemnification

Each party will defend and indemnify the other against third-party claims to the extent arising from its breach of this Agreement, negligence, or willful misconduct. Supplier will also defend Customer against third-party claims that the platform infringes intellectual property rights.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. These limits do not apply to breaches of confidentiality or to a party's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property claims.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New York County, New York.

## 11. General

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's consent, except to a successor in a merger or acquisition. This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

# Master Vendor Services Agreement

This Master Vendor Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 between Zephyr Placeholder Systems LLC ("Vendor") and Example Buyer Co. ("Customer"). All names in this document are fictitious.

## 1. Services

Vendor will provide the cloud analytics services described in each order form (the "Services"). Vendor may modify, suspend, or discontinue any feature of the Services at any time without notice to Customer.

## 2. Term and Automatic Renewal

The initial term is three (3) years. This Agreement renews automatically for successive three (3) year terms unless Customer gives written notice of non-renewal no earlier than 180 days and no later than 150 days before the end of the then-current term. Vendor may increase fees for any renewal term by any amount in its sole discretion.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Customer will pay all fees for the full initial term in advance, within seven (7) days of the invoice date. Overdue amounts accrue interest at 5% per month. All fees are non-refundable, including if Vendor fails to deliver the Services. Vendor may increase fees at any time during the term on 10 days' notice.

## 4. Termination

Vendor may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on 15 days' notice. Customer may not terminate this Agreement before the end of the then-current term for any reason, including Vendor's breach. If this Agreement ends for any reason other than Vendor's termination for convenience, all fees for the remainder of the term become immediately due.

## 5. Confidentiality

Customer will keep all Vendor information confidential for the term of this Agreement and for five (5) years after. Vendor has no confidentiality obligations to Customer, and may share Customer information with its affiliates, partners, and service providers as Vendor sees fit.

## 6. Intellectual Property

Vendor owns all deliverables, reports, and work product created under this Agreement, including any custom work paid for by Customer. Customer grants Vendor a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, sublicensable, royalty-free license to use, copy, modify, and commercialize Customer's data and any materials Customer provides, for any purpose.

## 7. Data Protection

Vendor may process Customer personal data in any country and may use it to improve Vendor's products, including training machine learning models. Vendor may engage subprocessors without notice to Customer. Vendor will notify Customer of a personal data breach when it considers notification commercially reasonable. Vendor may retain Customer data indefinitely after termination.

## 8. Indemnification

Customer will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor and its affiliates from any and all claims, losses, damages, and expenses arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, including those caused by Vendor's own negligence or willful misconduct. Customer's indemnity obligations are not subject to any limitation of liability.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Vendor's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed one hundred dollars ($100). In no event will Vendor be liable for any indirect, consequential, special, or lost-profit damages. Customer's liability under this Agreement is unlimited.

## 10. Non-Competition

During the term and for five (5) years after, Customer will not purchase analytics services from any competitor of Vendor anywhere in the world, and will not develop any product that competes with the Services.

## 11. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of a jurisdiction selected by Vendor. All disputes will be resolved by binding arbitration administered by an arbitrator chosen solely by Vendor. Customer waives any right to a jury trial and to participate in a class action.

## 12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements. Vendor may amend this Agreement by posting a new version on its website.
